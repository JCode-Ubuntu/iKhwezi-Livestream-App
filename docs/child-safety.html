Child Safety Standards

App Name: iKHWEZI
Last Updated: July 31, 2026
Website: https://ikhwezi.site

iKHWEZI is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards describe how we prevent, detect, and respond to child safety risks on our Service.

1. Age requirement

iKHWEZI is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts or use the Service. Accounts found to belong to users under 13 will be removed.

2. Zero tolerance for CSAE / CSAM

We strictly prohibit:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
  • Any content that sexualizes minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or exploitation of children
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing such content

Violations result in content removal, account termination, and reporting to relevant authorities where required by law.

3. Reporting child safety concerns

Anyone can report child safety concerns by emailing:

ikhweziapp@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Description of the concern
  • Username or profile link (if available)
  • Link, screenshot, or details of the content or behavior
  • Date/time if known

We review reports promptly and take action as appropriate.

4. Response and enforcement

When we become aware of CSAE/CSAM or child safety violations, we may:

  • Remove the content
  • Suspend or permanently ban the account
  • Preserve relevant records as required by law
  • Report to regional or national authorities / designated reporting organizations when legally required

5. Compliance

iKHWEZI complies with applicable child safety laws and Google Play child safety standards requirements for social apps. We maintain these published standards and a designated contact for child safety and CSAM prevention practices.
