This page explains how users of the iKHWEZI mobile app and website can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from the address linked to your iKHWEZI account (or include your registered username) to privacy@ikhwezi.site.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  3. In the message, include:
    • Your iKHWEZI username
    • The email address or phone number used to register
    • A clear statement that you want your account and personal data deleted
  4. We will confirm your request by email and begin processing deletion.
  5. Your account will be deactivated and personal data removed within 30 days of verification.

What Data Is Deleted

When your deletion request is approved and processed, we delete the following data associated with your account:

Data type Action
Account credentials (username, email, phone, password)Deleted
Profile information (display name, bio, profile picture)Deleted
Videos, photos, stories, and other content you uploadedDeleted
Comments, messages, and reactions linked to your accountDeleted
Followers, following, and social connectionsDeleted
Wallet balance, coins, gifts, and in-app purchase history tied to your accountDeleted
Usage and activity logs linked to your accountDeleted

What Data May Be Kept

Some information may be retained only where required by law, fraud prevention, or legitimate business needs:

Data type Action Retention period
Payment or transaction records required for tax, accounting, or legal complianceKeptUp to 5 years, where required by law
Security, abuse, or fraud investigation recordsKeptUp to 90 days after account deletion
Backups containing your dataRemoved on rolling scheduleUp to 30 days after deletion is processed
Content that other users saved, shared, or reposted outside your accountMay remainUntil removed by those users or as permitted by our policies

Retention Period

After we verify your identity, account deletion is completed within 30 days. Backup systems may take up to an additional 30 days to fully purge residual copies.
