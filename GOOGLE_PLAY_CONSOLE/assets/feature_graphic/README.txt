FOLDER: assets/feature_graphic/

WHAT TO PUT HERE: Your feature graphic (promotional banner)

SPECIFICATIONS:
- Filename: feature.png (or feature_graphic.png)
- Size: 1024×500 pixels (EXACTLY)
- Format: PNG
- Quantity: 1 image

WHAT IS A FEATURE GRAPHIC?
- A promotional banner for your app
- Shows up in Play Store featured sections
- Highlights app branding and key features
- First impression for potential users

DESIGN TIPS:
- Make it eye-catching
- Include app name/logo
- Show key features
- Use bright, contrasting colors
- Keep text readable at small sizes
- Include tagline (e.g., "Stream the night. Shine the signal.")
- Professional, polished look

WHAT TO SHOW:
- iKHWEZI branding/logo
- "Stream the night. Shine the signal." tagline
- Key feature icons or screenshots
- User demographic (young, creative, social)
- Call to action (optional)

HOW TO CREATE:
- Canva: Drag & drop design tool
- Photoshop / GIMP: Full control
- Figma: Online design tool
- AI Image generators: For quick mockups

HOW TO UPLOAD:
1. Google Play Console → App listing
2. Scroll to "Feature graphic"
3. Upload your 1024×500 feature.png
4. This appears on app store pages and promotions

FILE NAMING: feature.png
