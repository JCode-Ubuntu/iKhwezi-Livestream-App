FOLDER: assets/icon/

WHAT TO PUT HERE: Your app icon

SPECIFICATIONS:
- Filename: icon.png
- Size: 512×512 pixels
- Format: PNG
- Background: Can be transparent or solid

TIPS:
- Make it recognizable and clean
- Avoid text (or use minimal text)
- Use iKHWEZI branding/colors
- Keep it scalable
- Test on different phone home screens

HOW TO UPLOAD:
1. Google Play Console → App listing
2. Scroll to "App icon"
3. Upload your 512×512 icon.png
4. Google Play will generate smaller versions automatically

FILE NAMING: icon.png
