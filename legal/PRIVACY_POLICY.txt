PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: June 15, 2026

iKhwezi Hub ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect
- Account information: username, email, profile data.
- User content: media (videos, images) you upload.
- Usage data: logs, analytics, device and performance data.
- Optional: payment data via third-party providers (Stripe).

2. How We Use Data
We use data to operate and improve the App, deliver content, provide support, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Storage and Third Parties
We store media and assets in AWS S3, use RDS for relational data, ElastiCache for caching, and IVS/CloudFront for streaming/delivery. We may share data with service providers under contract.

4. Data Retention
We retain account and media data while your account exists and for a period thereafter as required for legal or business purposes. Users may request deletion; see Contact below.

5. Security
We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal data. No transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

6. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. To exercise rights, contact legal@ikhwezihub.co.za.

7. Children
The App is not intended for children under 13. If we learn a child under 13 has provided information, we will delete it.

8. Changes
We may update this policy; we will post changes and indicate the revision date.

Contact: legal@ikhwezihub.co.za
